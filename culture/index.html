AboutChidaoba

CHIDAOBA is a sports brand inspired by Georgian traditional wrestling. We support wrestling and grappling sports and the people who practise them.

UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, 2018

01 · The story of Chidaoba

01

Long before it was a sport, Chidaoba was preparation for battle, a skill it kept until the late Middle Ages.

02

Later it became a public sport. Tournaments were held in open-air arenas in villages and towns, in front of large crowds.

03

Bouts were accompanied by traditional music played on the zurna and the doli.

04

The rules were based on fairness and respect for the opponent and the audience.

05

The skills were passed down through families, schools and village clubs.

06

Today there is a wrestling club in almost every village and city in Georgia.

02 · Key facts

Key facts

2018

Inscribed on UNESCO’s Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

Open air

Tournaments in an outdoor arena, surrounded by a large audience.

Zurna & doli

Traditional wind and percussion instruments that accompany Chidaoba wrestling events.

Why the name Chidaoba

03 · The name

In Georgian, ჭიდაობა (Chidaoba) means wrestling. It is an everyday word that every Georgian knows.

We chose this name because it connects our brand to the Georgian wrestling tradition. Our goal is to make it known internationally.
