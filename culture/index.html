CHIDAOBA is a Georgian-rooted sports culture brand inspired by the tradition of wrestling. We celebrate where grappling comes from, support where it is going, and bring its community closer together.

UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity, 2018 Scroll

01 · The story of Chidaoba

01

Long before it was a sport, Chidaoba was preparation for battle, a skill it kept until the late Middle Ages.

Source: UNESCO

02

Then the fight became a celebration. Tournaments moved into open-air arenas in villages and towns, surrounded by large crowds.

Source: UNESCO; AFP, 2018

03

Music opened every bout. The zurna and the doli set the rhythm, the crowd formed the circle, and sometimes the wrestlers left the arena dancing.

Source: UNESCO

04

Its code was chivalric. Strength mattered, but so did respect: for the opponent, and for the people watching.

Source: UNESCO Decision 13.COM 10.b.15

05

It was passed from one generation to the next, in families, schools and village clubs. Children learned by watching, then practised outdoors from spring to autumn.

Source: UNESCO; AFP, 2018

06

Today there is a wrestling section in almost every village and city in Georgia. The tradition is still alive.

Source: UNESCO

03 · Why Chidaoba?

In Georgian, ჭიდაობა (Chidaoba) means wrestling. It is a familiar, everyday word, and one that carries generations of Georgian life, identity and tradition.

We chose it deliberately. CHIDAOBA takes that word beyond its traditional meaning and gives it a modern, international presence, so that a word every Georgian knows becomes a name the whole grappling world can recognise.

04 · More than one discipline

Inspired by heritage. Open to every grappler.

CHIDAOBA is inspired by Georgian heritage, but it is not limited to one traditional discipline. We support the sports that share its grip, its discipline and its values.
