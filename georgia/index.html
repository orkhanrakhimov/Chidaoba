Why Georgia

Why trainin Georgia

Georgia has a long wrestling tradition, world-class judo and wrestling athletes and a growing BJJ community. It is also an easy and affordable country to visit, with mountains, sea and great food.

01 · Roots

01

Wrestling has been part of Georgian life for centuries.

02

Chidaoba, the traditional Georgian wrestling, was used to prepare soldiers and later became a popular public sport.

03

Today almost every village and city has a wrestling club, and many children start training at a young age.

04

Training here means training in a country where these sports are taken seriously.

02 · Champions

Results on the international stage

Georgia has about 3.5 million people, but it is one of the most successful countries in the world in judo and wrestling.

7

Olympic medals at Paris 2024, Georgia's best Games as an independent country. Judo and wrestling led the way.

2025

World mixed team judo champions for the first time, ending Japan's run of seven straight titles.

23

World wrestling medals per million people, second only to Bulgaria in the all-time per capita ranking.

×2

Lasha Bekauri, Olympic judo champion in Tokyo and again in Paris. Geno Petriashvili took freestyle gold at 125 kg in Paris.

Even in the Soviet years, Georgian judoka won more medals for the USSR than any other republic. In 2026 Tbilisi hosted the European Judo Championships, and Georgia topped the table with four golds.

03 · BJJ and grappling

A growing BJJ community

Brazilian jiu-jitsu and no-gi grappling are growing quickly in Georgia. New academies are opening in Tbilisi, and international tournaments are now held in the city.

Many local athletes also have a wrestling or judo background, so training is technical and physically demanding.

04 · Nature and climate

Mountains, sea and good weather

Georgia is a small country with a lot of variety. The mountains and the Black Sea coast are both a few hours from Tbilisi.

The mountains

The Greater Caucasus runs along the north of the country, with peaks above 5,000 m. Mountain regions such as Svaneti stay cool in summer, which makes them good for training.

The sea

The Black Sea coast around Batumi has beaches and warm water in summer. A good option for rest days or after the camp.

The seasons

Summers are warm and sunny, spring and autumn are mild, and winters bring snow to the mountains. Most camps run from late spring to early autumn.

Food and wine

05 · Food

Georgian food is one of the best reasons to visit. A traditional feast is called a supra, with many dishes and toasts led by a tamada.

Georgia also has one of the oldest winemaking traditions in the world, about 8,000 years. Wine is still made in clay vessels called qvevri, a method listed by UNESCO.

Khinkali

Dumplings filled with spiced meat and broth.

Khachapuri

Bread baked with cheese. The Adjarian version is topped with egg and butter.

Mtsvadi

Grilled meat, traditionally cooked over vine branches.

Pkhali & badrijani

Vegetables with walnut paste, and fried aubergine rolls with walnut filling.

Lobio

Bean stew cooked in a clay pot, served with cornbread.

Churchkhela

Walnuts coated in thickened grape juice. A traditional snack.

06 · Hospitality

Hospitality

Georgians are known for their hospitality. There is a saying that a guest is a gift from God.

Visitors are often invited to share a meal or join a celebration. Traditional polyphonic singing, listed by UNESCO, is still part of many gatherings.

At our camps you train, eat and travel together as a group.

07 · Practical information

Practical information

Visa

Citizens of many countries, including the EU, UK and US, can stay without a visa for up to a year. Check the rules for your passport before you book.

Flights

International airports in Tbilisi, Kutaisi and Batumi, with direct routes from across Europe and the Middle East.

Money

The Georgian lari (GEL). Cards work almost everywhere in cities. Bring some cash for villages and markets.

Language

Georgian, with its own alphabet. Many young people speak English, and our team is with you the whole camp.

Best time

Late spring to early autumn for mountain camps. Tbilisi works all year.

Time zone

GMT+4, all year round.
