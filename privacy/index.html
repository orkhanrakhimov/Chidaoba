Privacy.

Last updated: November 2026

What this website collects

We only collect what you choose to send us through the forms on this site:

Camp interest form, name, email, country, age, training experience and the optional details you add (phone/WhatsApp, belt or skill level, club, interests, accommodation needs, message). Used to contact you about camps and events.

Contact form, name, email, topic, message and optional phone. Used to reply to you.

The Culture Letter, your email address. Used to send our newsletter; you can unsubscribe at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing us.

We don’t sell or share your details, and there are no accounts, analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

How the forms work

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox (chidaobaculture@gmail.com) by Web3Forms, a form-processing service. If the form opens your email app instead, your message is sent from your own email account. We keep your details only as long as needed. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email us and we’ll do it.

WhatsApp and Instagram links open those services; their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any web server, these services receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page or a font. Their own privacy policies apply.

Changes

When we add accounts or the shop, this page will be updated before any new personal data is collected.

Contact

Questions: email chidaobaculture@gmail.com or message us on Instagram at @chidaobaculture.
