Questions

FAQ

How do I sign up?

Choose your sport, open a camp, pick a package and press Request a spot. Fill in a short form and send it. We reply within 24 hours by WhatsApp or email to confirm your place.

Do I pay anything when I send a request?

No. Sending a request is free and does not commit you to anything. We confirm your spot and the payment details with you personally.

What if the camp is full?

Join the waitlist from the camp page. If a spot opens, we contact waitlisted people in order.

Do I need experience?

Every camp shows its level. Many are open to all levels; tell us your experience when you book.

Do I need to speak Georgian?

No. Sessions and communication for international guests are in English.

How do I get to Georgia?

Georgia has international airports in Tbilisi, Kutaisi and Batumi. Each camp page says where it is and whether transfers are available as an add-on. Please check the visa rules for your passport.

Can I bring my team?

Yes. Camps with a group discount show it on the camp page, and we can build a private camp for your club. Get in touch.
